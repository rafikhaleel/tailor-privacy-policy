TailorHub Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

TailorHub ("we", "our", or "the app") is a tailoring management application designed to help tailors manage customers, measurements, orders, payments, and related tailoring information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TailorHub collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you use TailorHub, we may collect and store the following information depending on the features you use:

  • Account name
  • Shop name, if provided
  • Email address
  • Preferred language
  • Preferred measurement unit, such as inches or centimeters
  • Customer names
  • Customer phone numbers
  • Customer gender, where provided
  • Customer profile photos, where provided
  • Customer notes
  • Body measurements
  • Custom measurement fields
  • Measurement history and revisions
  • Order information
  • Order prices and advance payments
  • Remaining balances and due dates
  • Order statuses and related tailoring information

2. Account Information

TailorHub uses email and password authentication for user accounts. Your email address and authentication information are used to create and maintain your TailorHub account and to provide access to your account data.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected by TailorHub is used to provide and improve the application's functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing your TailorHub account
  • Managing your customers
  • Recording and organizing measurements
  • Managing tailoring orders
  • Tracking payments and outstanding balances
  • Synchronizing your data across supported devices
  • Providing application settings and preferences
  • Maintaining and improving application reliability

4. Customer Data

TailorHub allows you to enter information about your customers, including names, phone numbers, photos, notes, measurements, and order information.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate permission or lawful basis to collect and use customer information that you enter into the application.

TailorHub uses customer information only as necessary to provide the application's tailoring-management features and related services.

5. Local Data Storage

TailorHub is designed to support offline use. Certain application data may be stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database.

Local data may remain on your device until it is deleted through the application or the application data is removed from the device.

6. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

TailorHub uses Supabase services for authentication, cloud database storage, and synchronization of supported application data.

Customer photos, when uploaded, may be stored using Supabase Storage.

Data transmitted to cloud services is used to provide account, synchronization, storage, and application functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

TailorHub uses third-party infrastructure services to provide certain application functionality.

  • Supabase — used for authentication, database services, data synchronization, and storage.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

8. Photos and Device Permissions

TailorHub may allow you to select or upload customer profile photos. Photos are used only as part of the customer-management functionality provided by the application.

If the application requests access to photos or other device features, the permission is used only for the functionality that requires it. You can manage application permissions through your device settings.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by TailorHub. Cloud data is transmitted to the services used by the application and access controls are used where applicable.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Deletion

TailorHub provides deletion functionality for certain records such as customers, measurements, and orders.

Some deleted records may be retained temporarily in backups, synchronization systems, or other technical storage before they are removed according to applicable retention processes.

Deleting a record from the application does not necessarily mean that every copy is immediately removed from all systems.

11. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your TailorHub account and associated personal data by submitting an account deletion request.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your TailorHub account, use the account deletion request form below.

Request Account Deletion

Please note that certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

12. Data Retention

We retain account and application data for as long as necessary to provide the requested services, maintain application functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

When deletion is requested, applicable data will be handled according to our deletion and retention processes.

13. Children's Privacy

TailorHub is intended for use by adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through TailorHub, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TailorHub, our services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

15. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the information you enter into TailorHub, including customer information, measurements, notes, photos, and order details.

Please ensure that the information you store in the application is collected and used in accordance with applicable laws and with any necessary customer permissions.
